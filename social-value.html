Armed Forces Covenant

Proudly supporting those who serve

YEC signed the Armed Forces Covenant on 12 March 2026, pledging support to the Armed Forces community and their families, and committing to fair and respectful treatment.

Our Social Value Champion engages with local Armed Forces charities and supports Armed Forces Day and Week, Reserves Day, the Poppy Appeal and Remembrance. We also give Reservists and Cadet Force Adult Volunteers in our employment additional leave for duties and training.

Community sponsorship
